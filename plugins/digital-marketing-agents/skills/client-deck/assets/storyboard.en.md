# Client deck storyboards

Choose one spine and write titles before building slides. Replace every angle-bracket placeholder with reconciled evidence or an explicit pending item. Write in the client's working language. Covers and section dividers orient; evidence slides make one claim each. Keep claim titles below about fifteen words and at most two lines. Repeat the actual asks and material uncertainty on slide 2.

For every chart put `Source: <source sheet/export>, <period>, <unit>` under it, with any material definition or attribution caveat. For tables and quoted findings add the corresponding source reference too. Show absolute and relative change against a named base period. Label preliminary figures visibly; if evidence is sparse, write "not enough data" and show counts or a table instead of a trend.

For every slide add notes with two likely objections, a one-sentence answer to each and a backup slide reference. The notes column below names useful objection topics, not a script. Put essential caveats on the slide itself. Keep internal notes in the working copy and prepare the intended client-facing version.

## Monthly performance report

| Slide | Title or claim to complete | Evidence and visible takeaway | Notes: objection topics |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | <Client>: <period> performance report | <systems, version, status, owner and report date> | Scope and period boundaries |
| 2 | <Main outcome> changed <amount>; approve <next action> | What happened, why, decision; <material miss and uncertainty>; <owner/date> | Why this decision now; what could change it |
| 3 | <Business outcome> reached <value>, <change> against <base period> | Exact results table or simple monthly panels; <absolute and relative change, target> | Outcome definition; attribution and lag |
| 4 | Budget and channel results | Section divider with <question this section answers> | What the section includes; what sits in appendix |
| 5 | <Channel> drove <share> of <outcome> in <period> | Ordered bars or exact table; <net/gross basis and source> | Cross-channel comparability; deduplication |
| 6 | <Metric> missed <target> by <amount> in <period> | Simple monthly panels, one highlighted bar; confirmed/suspected/unknown cause | Cause evidence; alternative explanation |
| 7 | <Funnel stage> lost <count>, limiting <business outcome> | Stage bars and step conversion; <sample size and definition> | Join loss; small base or delayed conversion |
| 8 | Spend used <share> of budget and changed cost by <amount> | Plan/actual table; same tax/fee and period basis; <absolute and relative variance> | Invoice reconciliation; variance owner |
| 9 | <Completed action> changed <metric> by <amount> | Live evidence or experiment result; state not done where applicable | Causality versus correlation; unfinished work |
| 10 | <Next action> targets <outcome> by <date> | Prioritised next-period actions, owners, evidence needed and forecast limits | Resource availability; stop rule |
| 11 | Approve <decision> by <date> to enable <outcome> | What we need from you: <one to three asks, owners, dates and delay impact> | Who can approve; dependency if delayed |
| 12 | Appendix: definitions and reconciliation | Index to <source register, complete results, methods and backup> | Where to find detail; source freshness |
| <appendix slides> | <One supporting claim or reference topic per slide> | <full tables, metric definitions, source filters, attribution limits, calculations> | <two likely objections, answers and backup references> |

Keep recurring monthly sections in the same order. Split a slide if two findings compete; remove an empty section rather than inventing an outcome. Use roughly 8-12 main slides as an editing starting point, not a required count. Do not chart three observations as a trend to fill a layout.

## Audit readout

| Slide | Title or claim to complete | Evidence and visible takeaway | Notes: objection topics |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | <Client>: <scope> audit readout | <systems, audit period, version, status, owner and date> | Access boundaries; tested period |
| 2 | <Verified gap> blocks <decision>; approve <remediation> | What happened, why, required decision; <material unknowns>; <owner/date> | Why this finding matters; confidence |
| 3 | We verified <coverage> of <scope> during <period> | Scope matrix: checked, not checked, inaccessible; <method and limitations> | Sampling; untested areas |
| 4 | Evidence and business impact | Section divider with <decision blocked by the findings> | Priority logic; impact basis |
| 5 | <Failure> affects <verified count/share> of <flow> | Expected versus observed evidence; <source and period> | Reproduction; sample representativeness |
| 6 | <Second gap> prevents <specific measurement or action> | One gap, source evidence, quantified exposure if supported | Alternative cause; duplicated finding |
| 7 | <Root cause> explains <verified part> of the gap | Flow or mapping table; confirmed/suspected/unknown distinctions | Causal evidence; unresolved joins |
| 8 | Fix <priority> first to restore <verifiable outcome> | Remediation table: priority, owner, dependency, effort basis, acceptance and rollback | Effort uncertainty; release constraints |
| 9 | <Test> will verify <acceptance norm> before launch | Verification plan and staged exit conditions; <evidence owner/date> | Production safety; acceptance evidence |
| 10 | Assign <owners> by <date> to start <remediation> | What we need from you: <decisions, access, owners, dates and delay impact> | Access approval; scope if dependency is absent |
| 11 | Appendix: evidence and methods | Index to <test records, payload placeholders, queries and source register> | Reproducibility; source limitations |
| <appendix slides> | <One supporting claim or reference topic per slide> | <full evidence, definitions, exclusions and reproduction steps without secrets or personal data> | <two likely objections, answers and backup references> |

Do not turn an unverified financial exposure into a loss claim. Label impact estimates and their method. Keep not checked distinct from passed. Put implementation contracts in a linked dev-spec rather than burying the handoff in the deck.

## Proposal

| Slide | Title or claim to complete | Evidence and visible takeaway | Notes: objection topics |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | <Client>: proposal for <outcome> | <scope, version, status, owner and date> | Proposal boundaries; intended approver |
| 2 | Approve <scope> to pursue <target> by <date> | Recommendation, why, decision; <forecast uncertainty>; <approval owner/date> | Why this option; forecast versus guarantee |
| 3 | <Problem> affected <verified metric> during <period> | Sourced baseline and business consequence; <known limitations> | Baseline reliability; cost of doing nothing |
| 4 | Recommended scope and alternatives | Section divider with <decision criterion> | Evaluation criteria; stakeholder needs |
| 5 | <Recommended option> best meets <agreed criterion> | Comparable options table, trade-offs and evidence; include current-state option | Rejected alternatives; hidden dependencies |
| 6 | <Scope> delivers <verifiable outputs> within <boundary> | Deliverables, acceptance, exclusions and client responsibilities | Scope creep; what the client must supply |
| 7 | <Mechanism> can improve <metric> under <assumptions> | Logic and source-based scenario table; label targets and estimates | Causality; sensitivity to assumptions |
| 8 | <Stages> reach <milestone> by <conditional date> | Stages, owners, dependencies, exit conditions and rollback | Schedule feasibility; missing dependency |
| 9 | <Budget> funds <scope> on <commercial basis> | Exact net/fee/tax/gross table, currency, period and sources; <included/excluded costs> | Invoice basis; procurement comparison |
| 10 | <Risk response> limits <identified exposure> | Risk, likelihood basis, impact, mitigation, owner and decision gate | Security or privacy; residual risk |
| 11 | Approve <scope and budget> by <date> to begin <stage> | What we need from you: <one to three decisions, owners, dates and next step> | Approval authority; consequences of delay |
| 12 | Appendix: assumptions and supporting detail | Index to <sources, full scenarios, definitions and proposed contracts> | Evidence freshness; commercial assumptions |
| <appendix slides> | <One supporting claim or reference topic per slide> | <full calculations, option comparison and implementation detail without secrets or personal data> | <two likely objections, answers and backup references> |

Keep expected outcomes separate from contractual deliverables. Use a table for exact commercial numbers and show their source, period and tax basis. Link the media-plan or dev-spec when they own the approval or implementation detail.
