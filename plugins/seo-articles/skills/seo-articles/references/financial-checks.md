# Financial content checks

For pages that state rates, fees, terms, credit scores or lending rules. Reopen the primary instrument for every revision and note the date you checked it; do not infer a legal rule from this digest alone.

## Sources and scope

- Cite the regulator's own text (circular, directions, statute) with its date and the paragraphs you rely on. State exceptions; do not turn a general rule into an absolute.
- A registry or directory listing is not regulator endorsement unless the instrument says so.
- Credit bureaus: take the score range and the meaning of "no history" from the bureau's own pages. The lender, not the bureau, decides on a loan. Do not invent approval thresholds or probability claims.
- Free credit reports: state the frequency and terms the bureau publishes; paid products are separate.

India as an example, checked 11 October 2026 (keep other jurisdictions in your overlay):
- RBI, Key Facts Statement (KFS) for Loans & Advances, 15 April 2024, RBI/2024-25/18: https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12663&Mode=0. Paragraphs 4-9 cover a KFS in a language the borrower understands, its validity period, the APR computation sheet and repayment schedule, third-party charges counted in the APR and shown separately, no charge outside the KFS without the borrower's explicit consent, and the KFS as a summary box in the loan agreement. Paragraph 11 applies it to all new retail and MSME term loans sanctioned on or after 1 October 2024.
- Reserve Bank of India (Digital Lending) Directions, 2025, 8 May 2025, RBI/2025-26/36: https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12848&Mode=0. Paragraphs 6-13 cover offers from multiple lenders, creditworthiness, disclosures, fund flows, the cooling-off period, grievance redressal, data consent and data storage. Paragraph 17(vi): reporting a digital lending app to the RBI is not registration, authorization or endorsement by the Reserve Bank, and must not be presented as such in marketing.
- TransUnion CIBIL (https://www.cibil.com/faq/credit-score-and-loan-basics, https://www.cibil.com/freecibilscore): the score ranges from 300 to 900; NA or NH means too little credit history or recent activity to be scored, not a negative mark; one free score and report per calendar year; the decision to lend rests with the lender.

## Computation

Keep APR separate from an annual nominal interest rate. `principal × rate × days / 365` is simple interest on unchanged principal, not a general APR-to-cost conversion. A fee cannot simply be added above an all-in APR cap while still labeling the result compliant with that cap.

For equal monthly instalments use `EMI = P*r*(1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n-1)`, where `r = annual nominal percentage / 1200`; for zero rate use `P/n`. Calculate totals before rounding. Disclose principal, rate basis, repayment timing, term, omitted fees and rounding. Illustrative nominal-rate examples are not offers or an APR calculation. A calendar month is not a fixed 30 days, so a term stated in days and one stated in months are not interchangeable.

For fee deductions, distinguish sanctioned principal, net cash received and total repayments. APR requires dated cash flows and prescribed methodology. Do not manufacture it by adding fee percentages or multiplying interest by a factor.

Use an affordability ratio only as a stated modeling assumption, never a universal lender rule or guarantee. Essential spending, income volatility and emergency reserves still matter. Do not call a budget calculation lender eligibility.

## Product truth

State the product's limits as the site states them, and say that actual offers and the provider's own terms control. Describe how offers are ordered only from the code that orders them.
